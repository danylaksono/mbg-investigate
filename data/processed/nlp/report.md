# NLP exploration of the MBG corpus

Generated by `analysis/nlp_explore.py`. 308 documents, 296 linked to exactly one event (group comparisons use those). Coverage: 601 of 842 table rows have a readable article.

## How to read this

- Most articles are written within a day or two of the incident (section 5), so "awaiting lab" reflects *when the article was written*, not that no cause was ever found. Nothing here says what the eventual lab results were.
- Symptom and food shares are what reporters chose to mention, not clinical incidence.
- Lexicon precision was checked by reading matches (`--examples`); recall was not measured, so the small lab-result counts are floors.
- Differences are only worth quoting if they survive the Bonferroni column in section 6.
